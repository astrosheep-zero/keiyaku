# CLI Output

CLI Output owns help composition, shared rendering, and process outcome
projection. It consumes typed public values only. It does not reread
authority, change an owner's judgment, or make a display convenience durable.

## Help and outcome

Root help is a compact index of product pillars and repository utilities.
Each command family supplies its own purpose and leaf help; leaf help is the
sole user-facing owner of literal invocation grammar. Help remains available
without a World, input stream, or product observation. A syntax or input-source
refusal retains its specific cause and exposes the smallest applicable usage
and help handles without trying the command or appending a complete help page.
Explicit help remains complete, including supported invocation coordinates.

Process outcomes form a fixed six-way classification -- accepted, unsatisfied,
refused, retryable, failed, usage -- shared with other invocation adapters by the
facet. An unsatisfied verdict answers the requested check negatively; it remains
distinct from a failure to complete the operation.
The CLI owns its numeric exit projection; changing either the classification or
its exit mapping is a product change, and ordinary receipt text never carries
the numeric map. Final text rendering is shared, pure projection of native
answers and explicitly supplied presentation context.

Substantive refusals use one text grammar: a denied-verdict title naming the
verb, a labeled reason with machine kinds rendered as words, and
labeled entity/state facts where present. Usage refusals retain their compact
usage grammar. Operation and recovery handles do not prescribe a working
directory; any workspace coordinate is a separate fact for the caller to use.

Text is the primary readable projection and JSON is the complete typed
projection of the same result. Rendering retains the meaningful distinctions
between success, substantive refusal, retryable conflict, absent authority,
and operational failure. Text labels name a candidate and its integration result,
and a content identity is distinguished from a commit by its kind name alone —
the teaching that a content identity is never Git-addressable lives in leaf
help, not on receipt rows. Journal blobs, entry ids, and record-field
enumeration stay out of ordinary text. Ordinary JSON receipts likewise omit
raw journal records and internal custody; explicitly requested full history
retains historical content without duplicating storage envelopes. Verification
evidence remains associated with its exact
captured snapshot and individual requirement: a verify receipt exposes every
executed check's verdict or stop, current declaration position, requirement, and
admission reuse or stop when recording is selected. Ungated execution explicitly
states that no testimony was recorded; this is neither a failure nor an admission
stop. Delivery and review receipts consume standing evidence without claiming a
producer run. When a
verified snapshot lands, the landing names that same identity and its destination
movement. Successful internal retirement is not an ordinary receipt inventory;
failed removal retains its diagnostic path.
A landed placement is never vetoed by uncommitted checkout state: the
receipt names each target checkout this command's own follow could not carry as
one `checkout behind` lag row with its path and target, leaving earlier
retention to the typed result.

A completed placement names a Contract destination by its complete identity,
not by its private Git reference or another branch, and reads like Git movement
identically for review, delivery, and verify: the title states the outcome and
Contract, the movement names its reference, and the final lifecycle state reads
`accepted`. Receipt Git identities are abbreviated in text by the shared
collision-aware display rule and remain full in JSON; absent content identity
has no row. Amend receipts use a verb-past title, name gate and prerequisite
changes independently, reserve an unchanged-terms fact for a genuinely unchanged
amendment, and summarize actually changed sections instead of printing a full
terms diff. Debt mutation receipts use verb-past titles and field-level changes, never
projection-file diffs; Debt show timestamps omit millisecond precision in text
and retain full precision in JSON. A placement receipt has three registers:
landed states the reference movement and closes on `accepted`; pending states
the target's non-movement and closes on `⧗ awaiting <events>` (omitted when
every unmet requirement already holds a recorded verdict, which alarms on its
own row); and failed keeps its stop rows. Placement names its final integration
after any necessary reintegration. Requirement states are met or unmet
while recorded verdicts are satisfied or unsatisfied. A dependent stop on unmet
requirements speaks the same pending vocabulary. Internal refusal kinds never
print on receipts.

A birth or admission receipt takes the admitted fact's native grammar and stops
at that fact: a timeline admission uses a row, and Git movement uses a reference
line. A frame rule appears only when content follows; no admission receipt adds
an observation conclusion or claims a live state. Reset receipts enumerate
owner-confirmed removals and explain retained coordination residue without
claiming a world-wide transaction.

## Shared rendering

Domain-state marks and refusal receipt assembly each have one definition site.
State surfaces show requirement fill against the current candidate; event
surfaces use bare verdict marks. A settled successful action spends no mark;
failure, unresolved activity, and witnessed motion remain distinct. Only a
redrawable live frame may assert continuing liveness. Voice and input carry
their own marks. History never asserts current motion.

Renderers show complete public identity and decision-relevant evidence without
inventing status, hiding section failure as zero, or turning unknown evidence
into a positive result. The text vocabulary is closed and owned once by the
shared renderer: it distinguishes presence, temporal waiting, structural
waiting on a dependency, affirmed and denied verdicts, attention, and the
unknown — waiting is not absence, and an unsatisfied gate is not generic
attention. Facts are labeled human facts, joined with one separator; key-value
notation, banners, and bracket state alphabets are not text vocabulary. Git
identities are short in text and full in JSON. Paths stay absolute and
copyable where they are the answer, and stay out of board and catalogue rows
where they are not. An empty standalone catalogue names its surface once.
Empty World sections omit their header; a wholly empty World retains its shared
empty state. Empty views print no observed timestamp, count, or container.
The whoami identity observation leads with self and names requested
absence and unknowns explicitly. Non-Akuma identity is marked as such and omits
Akuma-only details. The Akuma head carries identity, aliases, Dispatch and the
execution directory when known, as in status. Its current initiator, birth
caller, context, and Dispatch remain distinct; text does not repeat context
when it matches Dispatch, or show birth time or direct origin.
JSON retains the complete typed facts, including those omitted from text.

A bounded catalogue signals its one public fact — that more rows exist —
without adding counts, an exhaustive-mode claim, or a continuation
instruction; the omission marker renders bounded observation, not a recovery
route. A complete catalogue has no omission tail. Multi-line payloads sit
directly under their label, bounded, and name their own truncation. A
caller-scoped Akuma catalogue names a descendant row's immediate calling
Akuma when the resolved selected caller is available to the renderer; a direct
child of that caller needs no such label. When the selected caller is unavailable,
the label is omitted without another observation or a new result envelope.
Ordinary catalogue rows keep their shape.

Kanshi owns what a composite report means; this chapter owns its terminal
projection. Debt owns Debt facts and [cli-debt.md](cli-debt.md) owns their
Debt-facing presentation. Contract, Git, and Akuma renderers receive their
facts from their respective public results. Activity summaries retain the
concrete work observed rather than substituting a broad category; a tool row
names the actual tool observed with a bounded argument preview, and a partial
capture truncates visibly. Rendering does not interpret foreign command or
parameter dialects: retained commands and paths use the shared bounded
payload discipline. Native tool rows preserve a nonempty work preview and the
semantic trailer of read ranges, diffstats, durations, and exit/error status
within the terminal width. Status and World render the native owner's concrete
current-action summary with its retained coordinate and visible clipping;
renderers never reconstruct tool state or expand omitted activity. Native history
keeps observed result diagnostics, expanding a cut inline diagnostic into a
bounded continuation block. Diagnostics that fit are shown whole; longer
captures name their omission. Gate evidence keeps its compact visual
state beside each gate and requirement's identity across status, board, history,
preview, receipts, and JSON; current declarations may be labeled by current one-based
position while removed historical requirements retain stable identity. A raw
caller answer remains raw when that operation promises answer bytes and has no
meaningful failure, control, or recovery evidence. Otherwise the shared receipt
presents the answer together with confirmed admission, association, wake,
observation, and recovery evidence. An answered Ask's accepted judgment does not
hide its historical wake diagnostic. Recording
review testimony and completing placement remain distinct facts; absence of
completion does not establish a candidate.

Roster, world-status, and debt rows state each fact once in words. A Contract
association prints as the bare Contract id inline; a free row states no
association at all; the association carries a parenthetical only when the read
established a reason a person can act on. A row prints its activity age only
when it differs from the state age. Activity previews keep observed source
punctuation, apply only terminal-safe handling, whitespace folding, and
width-aware truncation, and never rewrite what an Akuma said. Width is counted
in terminal cells for whole grapheme clusters. Debt dispositions render as
words wherever debt rows appear; the stored dropping state reads as dropped.

Known Akuma selection conditions render as one compact caller fact naming the
coordinate and the useful relation; they do not expose Heart birth terminology,
observation carrier words, or field labels. A failure to observe an
established Akuma keeps its reason with that identity. Pre-operation refusals
remain distinct from provider, lifecycle, and action failures after the target
has been established.

One Akuma action family covers spawns, forks, tells, and kills, including plural
and partial outcomes. Receipts lead with the action verdict and complete Akuma
identity; aliases supplement identity. Association, source, execution directory,
submitted input, and diagnostics remain distinct supporting facts. No receipt
claims unqualified success over a failed stage. A Tell proves admission and
delivery, never work finished; its input and admitted child survive a later
failure, alongside the native cause and known recovery coordinates. Empty input
adds nothing and held input stays distinct from failed delivery. Kill preserves
each member's native outcome in frozen selection order instead of collapsing
partial failures into one verdict.

Opaque configuration and observation values retain exact names, scalar types,
collection membership, empty values, and provenance. Terminal-safe escaping is
reversible. Refusal and recovery text keeps concrete causes, capabilities, and
coordinates. Presentation may wrap or densify evidence but cannot truncate a
copyable identity, replace a public discriminant with decoration, or remove
evidence merely to hide internal terminology.

One Akuma observation discipline keeps complete identity and known static
context ahead of current work or chronological activity, followed by the native
life judgment, reported changes, and diagnostics. Status renders semantic work;
history and live observation retain their chronological interpretation.
Known coordinates appear once; unknown coordinates stay absent. Selected Akuma
status foregrounds the age of its latest retained activity, when any, without
inferring activity from a Turn opening or claiming continuous liveness. The
projection owner supplies semantic current-work facts and one concrete bounded
current-action summary, with exact retained detail coordinates. Status and World
render that answer without expanding a chat timeline. Thin catalogue rows share
one projection with World observation; World current work is a separate bounded
native observation. History and live observation retain their chronological
activity, omissions and complete native interpretation. Changes within the execution
directory have priority over outside changes, which are summarized separately;
an unavailable directory preserves the
unpartitioned summary. Identity, conclusion, reported changes, and actionable
failure or pending evidence remain visible. A diagnostic keeps its subject,
unavailable state, cause, and recovery coordinates together, never as an
unlabeled static property. Activity retains the owner's event order, selection,
omissions, payloads, and attribution. A conclusion already supplied by the
timeline is not repeated.

History is immutable: it neither fabricates current state nor reads extra state
to fill its panel. Status consumes the same completion judgment as wait,
including outstanding input on a sleeping Akuma. Only an observed input wait
justifies elapsed-duration text; unknown change size never implies additions or
removals. Activity omits wall-clock timestamps while retaining witnessed tool
and wait durations.

Free text shares one bounded payload discipline across Contract testimony and
Akuma observations. Multiline payloads preserve paragraphs and indentation;
single-line previews fold whitespace. Speech, input, thought, and action remain
distinct without invented quotation marks, and wrapping never changes event
selection or meaning. Whole-grapheme terminal width governs wrapping and visible
truncation, preferring word boundaries. History previews retain the beginning
and name their omission; status and World carry outcome coordinates and verdicts
rather than answer previews. An exact
activity text read returns the full retained authored content or neutral tool
summary without terminal framing or payload clipping; exact typed output keeps
the complete retained native row. Paged Akuma history exposes each retained
report and Turn outcome's exact address directly, keeping those coordinates
copyable even in a narrow terminal. Outcome payloads follow their address and
preserve multiline relative indentation. Live observation keeps its existing
presentation; exact item and latest-answer reads remain raw retained bytes.
Capture and retention limits remain honest.

Selected Contract status foregrounds current work or the terminal outcome,
without repeating settled mechanics. A delivered integration observed before
target movement is identified as such. Delivery preview identifies itself as a
dry-run observation, never delivery success. Candidate preparation, standing
requirement evidence, and target followability stay distinct. Known checkout
non-followability retains its path and cause without becoming a target-reference
placement refusal; target lag remains counted or explicitly unknown.

Progress notices, installation reports, diagnostics, and completion receipts
are ephemeral process observations, useful only when they report an actual
boundary — never simulated progress, persisted facts, or a hidden alternative
protocol. Four laws govern them. A command's durable output is one receipt,
plus refusals; progress is not durable output. Progress lives only in the
redrawable live frame and vanishes at completion, except that verification's
own stdout and stderr content is factual and may stream. Off a terminal only
completed facts appear — phase-finish lines and bounded live output, never a
phase start, a stage transition, or an admitted fact. Admitted facts are named
exactly once, in the receipt: a failure receipt names the facts admitted before
the failure and then names the failure in outcome vocabulary with its reason,
and internal phase names never appear in receipt text. A stop on a retryable
conflict names its retry class in that same outcome vocabulary, never a
terminal or substantive mark for a stop that concluded nothing. For delivery,
review, verify, and delivery preview, witnessed progress lives on the
progress channel while one complete final result remains on stdout; on a
terminal the only liveness evidence is a ticking status line, and a quiet
interval never claims that a command is hung or alive. Terminal cancellation
asks the owned operation to stop and waits for its truthful final receipt
rather than replacing it with an early exit. Verification observations name the
current declaration position, requirement, and exact snapshot without becoming a
second result model. Its final receipt preserves partial admissions, retained
failure scenes, operational stops, and the ordinary completion judgment.

An observing spawn, an observing tell, and an ask share one input-wait
presentation discipline. Witnessed timeline activity streams once in owner order on the
progress channel; active work stays in the redrawable frame until it settles.
Closing preserves unresolved activity rather than inventing completion.
Observing tell begins at its own admitted input, leaving earlier work to history.
Its answer belongs to that
input's exact terminal Turn. Stdout carries text answer bytes for tell and spawn,
and serialized JSON for ask, once; the verb determines that projection. Deadline and failure preserve the observed life and explicit
cause without fabricating an answer. Bounded omissions remain visible, settled
rows never replay, and failures are never hidden by compaction.

A wait is not a stream. It observes each selected Akuma to its completion
judgment or the caller deadline and then prints one compact result line per
member in result order on stdout: an observed life mark, complete identity with
any single-target alias as a parenthetical, and the final public snapshot's
summary folded and bounded to the terminal without truncating identity. The
summary names a stopped life even when an older answer exists, keeps pending
input and a resume-unsupported strand visible, and otherwise reads the current
answered preview, failure diagnostic, or completion; a running member stays
running. Members the wait could not read follow as one failure line each with
complete identity and diagnostic. A wait opens no live frame and redraws no
progress.

Region overlap in bind and amend receipts remains supporting evidence, not a
warning or a separate report; it groups by related Contract and renders
identity, containment, and partial intersection relations without asserting a
code conflict. When an exceptional Contract execution already confirmed
admissions, the CLI reports the failure together with those receipts instead
of projecting a usage error or a no-effect refusal. Text and JSON preserve the
same confirmed admission, consequential movement, and failure boundary without
copying entire journal records. Resource issues keep enough ownership coordinates
to distinguish problems from different candidates or dependents; successful
internal mechanics and no-op inventories are not ordinary receipt content.

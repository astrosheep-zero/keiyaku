# CLI Output

CLI Output owns help composition, shared rendering, and process outcome
projection. It consumes typed public values only. It does not reread
authority, change an owner's judgment, or make a display convenience durable.

## Help and outcome

Root help is a compact index of product pillars and repository utilities.
Each command family supplies its own purpose and leaf help; leaf help is the
sole user-facing owner of literal invocation grammar. Help remains available
without a World, input stream, or product observation. A syntax or input-source
refusal retains its specific cause and exposes the smallest applicable usage
and help handles without trying the command or appending a complete help page.
Explicit help remains complete, including supported invocation coordinates.

Process outcomes use the invocation facet's shared classification. An
unsatisfied verdict answers the requested check negatively; incomplete
observation and failure to complete the operation remain distinct.
The CLI owns its numeric exit projection; changing either the classification or
its exit mapping is a product change, and ordinary receipt text never carries
the numeric map. Final text rendering is shared, pure projection of native
answers and supplied presentation context. Native recovery facts and diagnostics
are syntax-neutral. Render a recovery call only when the surface can preserve
the repository context, addressed subject, and intent; otherwise show the evidence alone.

Contract-family receipts lead with the requested operation, the invocation owner's
disposition, and the complete addressed identity when known. Review keeps
the supplied verdict; preview names its dry-run intent and conflict handoff names
its distinct conclusion. Leading admission and final placement remain independent:
a failed invocation can still retain admitted work and landed movement. Rendering
never judges eligibility, currentness, severity, or completion again.

One sparse composition selects workspace and target, semantic term changes,
chapter, candidate and integration and content identities, checks, placement,
attempted dependents, overlap observations, issues, and recovery coordinates in
that order, omitting absent collections. Contract-family identities and paths stay
complete regardless of terminal width. Candidate commits, integration results,
content identities, and captured snapshots retain distinct labels. Placement
names its destination and before/after movement before its native conclusion;
an unplaced result never claims Contract acceptance or invents an awaiting event.
Requirement evidence retains the gate, stable identity and supplied current
position, native missing, stale or unsatisfied state, stale reason, and summary.
Prerequisites retain identity and native state. Checks retain declaration
position, verdict or execution stop, and admission conclusion. Execution without
recording, admitted testimony, reused testimony, and stopped recording remain
distinct; delivery and review never imply a check ran.

Stops and issues share native kinds as words and present labeled coordinates,
reasons and the complete supplied diagnostics. Receipt rendering does not
truncate diagnostic or recovery text, including when the producer supplies
recovery prose in a diagnostic rather than a separate fact. Native recovery
facts remain separate and complete; the renderer never infers a recovery call
from prose. Every required issue and each optional issue
carrying a path, recovery coordinate or diagnostic remains visible across ordinary,
preview, terminal and exceptional receipts. Optional residue never changes the
invocation disposition. Evidence stays in producer order, including repeats;
rendering adds no cross-evidence suppression or deduplication. Reconciliation
and exceptional receipts share physical effect presentation. Known admissions,
attempted dependent outcomes, reference and worktree movement, retained scratch,
recovery snapshots and underlying causes survive trailing errors or cancellation.
Concrete recovery calls and bind draft preservation remain visible without
capture or staging tutorials, which belong to leaf help.

Text is the primary readable projection and JSON remains the complete typed
projection of the same result. Journal blobs, entry ids, and storage envelopes
stay out of ordinary text. Historical attestation subjects retain their labeled
semantic facts; unrecognized representations remain literal terminal-safe
evidence. Contract history retains the same semantic record in page and exact
JSON without duplicating storage envelopes. Debt mutation receipts use
verb-past titles and field-level changes; Debt show timestamps omit millisecond
precision in text and retain full precision in JSON.

A birth or admission receipt takes the admitted fact's native grammar and stops
at that fact: a timeline admission uses a row, and Git movement uses a reference
line. No admission receipt adds an observation conclusion or claims a live
state. Reset receipts enumerate owner-confirmed removals and explain retained
coordination residue without claiming a world-wide transaction.

## Shared rendering

Domain-state marks and refusal receipt assembly each have one definition site.
State surfaces report current requirement evidence; historical testimony uses
bare verdict marks. A settled successful action spends no mark;
failure, unresolved activity, and witnessed motion remain distinct. Only a
redrawable live frame may assert continuing liveness. Voice and input carry
their own marks. History never asserts current motion.

Renderers show complete public identity and decision-relevant evidence without
inventing status, hiding section failure as zero, or turning unknown evidence
into a positive result. The text vocabulary is closed and owned once by the
shared renderer: it distinguishes presence, temporal waiting, structural
waiting on a dependency, affirmed and denied verdicts, attention, and the
unknown — waiting is not absence, and an unsatisfied gate is not generic
attention. Facts are labeled human facts, joined with one separator; key-value
notation, banners, and bracket state alphabets are not text vocabulary. Outside
the Contract result family, Git identities use the shared short display in text
and remain full in JSON. Paths stay absolute and
copyable where they are the answer, and stay out of board and catalogue rows
where they are not. An empty standalone catalogue names its surface once.
Diagnostic-free empty World sections omit their header; a wholly empty World retains its shared
empty state. Empty views print no observed timestamp, count, or container.
The whoami identity observation leads with self and names requested
absence and unknowns explicitly. Non-Akuma identity is marked as such and omits
Akuma-only details. The Akuma head carries identity, aliases, Dispatch and the
execution directory when known, as in status. Its current initiator, birth
caller, context, and Dispatch remain distinct; text does not repeat context
when it matches Dispatch, or show birth time or direct origin.
JSON retains the complete typed facts, including those omitted from text.

A bounded catalogue signals its one public fact — that more rows exist —
without adding counts, an exhaustive-mode claim, or a continuation
instruction; the omission marker renders bounded observation, not a recovery
route. A complete catalogue has no omission tail. Multi-line payloads sit
directly under their label, bounded, and name their own truncation. A
caller-scoped Akuma catalogue names a descendant row's immediate calling
Akuma when the resolved selected caller is available to the renderer; a direct
child of that caller needs no such label. When the selected caller is unavailable,
the label is omitted without another observation or a new result envelope.
Ordinary catalogue rows keep their shape.

Kanshi owns what a composite report means; this chapter owns its terminal
projection. The active-work summary states the independent section totals and
availability without rendering any roster or expanding the current appointment.
Incomplete conflict evidence stays visible beside any known conflicts, never as
a zero total. A present empty section reports zero in this counting view.
World presents independent flat Contract, Akuma, and Debt sections
in that order, preserving each owner's native row order and catalogue aperture.
World and standalone catalogues share each family's thin row projection. An
Akuma's supplied Contract association stays inline with its complete identity,
including links outside the displayed Contract page. Availability and bounded
omissions remain visible independently. Alias failure is stated once for the
section, not for each member; addressed status retains local Alias evidence.
A present empty section omits its header unless it carries a diagnostic; absent
and unavailable sections remain explicit. Catalogue rows use one layout across
terminal widths and state lifecycle, delivery, and native gate verdicts in words.
They add no aggregate alarm or age-based tone judgment.
Debt owns Debt facts and [cli-debt.md](cli-debt.md) owns their
Debt-facing presentation. Contract, Git, and Akuma renderers receive their
facts from their respective public results. Recent Akuma activity uses the
owner's shared row wording. Renderers present supplied snapshot text without
reconstructing activity or applying a second aperture; exact presentation shares
the neutral wording without imposing the snapshot bound. A tool row names the
actual tool observed with a bounded argument preview, and a partial
capture truncates visibly. Rendering does not interpret foreign command or
parameter dialects: observed arguments use the shared bounded preview while
copyable paths stay whole. Native tool rows preserve the work preview and expose
read ranges, diffstats, and observed exit or error status as separate facts.
Tool starts and completions remain separate retained observations; rendering
derives no duration from their timestamps. A row whose provider supplied no preview
carries the tool identity alone; the truncation mark means a partial capture,
never a missing one, and an explicitly empty argument object stays visibly
empty. Status and World render the native owner's already bounded current-action
summary as supplied, with terminal-safe handling, its complete retained activity
coordinate, and the owner's clipping intact. They do not clip it again,
reconstruct tool state, or expand omitted activity. Exact retained activity
remains separately addressed through history. Native history keeps observed
result diagnostics in a separate bounded cause. Diagnostics within the preview
aperture are shown whole; longer or partial captures name their omission.
Status and catalogue rows state gate evidence in words beside the gate and
requirement identity. Contract receipts and previews
retain the native requirement evidence described above; JSON retains native
values. Current declarations may be labeled by current one-based position while
removed historical requirements retain stable identity. A raw
caller answer remains raw when that operation promises answer bytes and has no
meaningful failure, control, or recovery evidence. Otherwise the shared receipt
presents the answer together with confirmed admission, association, wake,
observation, and recovery evidence. An answered Ask's accepted judgment does not
hide its historical wake diagnostic. Recording
review testimony and completing placement remain distinct facts; absence of
completion does not establish a candidate.

Roster, world-status, and debt rows state each fact once in words. Native pending
input coordinates take precedence over a catalogue count of the same evidence.
A Contract association prints as the bare Contract id inline; a free row states no
association at all; the association carries a parenthetical only when the read
established a reason a person can act on. A row prints its activity age only
when it differs from the state age. Activity previews keep observed source
punctuation and never rewrite what an Akuma said. The aperture for previews of
raw content is fixed in source lines and whole graphemes, independent of
terminal width. It makes both presentation omission and owner-reported partial
capture visible without treating authored punctuation as evidence of omission. Debt
dispositions render as words wherever debt rows appear; the stored dropping
state reads as dropped.

Known Akuma selection conditions render as one compact caller fact naming the
coordinate and the useful relation; they do not expose Heart birth terminology,
observation carrier words, or field labels. A failure to observe an
established Akuma keeps its reason with that identity. Pre-operation refusals
remain distinct from provider, lifecycle, and action failures after the target
has been established. A Tell refusal for a missing frozen directory names the
existing Akuma and exact directory, without claiming input was admitted or a
new birth failed.

One Akuma action family covers spawns, forks, tells, and kills, including plural
and partial outcomes. Receipts lead with the action verdict and complete Akuma
identity; aliases supplement identity. A partial Spawn instead leads with the
born child, the stage that stopped, and a warning against respawning. Returned
and exceptional partials share this presentation and keep the failed process
outcome. Requested and confirmed associations stay distinct. A Tell recovery
call requires proof the original input was not admitted, and must preserve the
World, exact child and input; otherwise the receipt shows evidence alone.
Association, source, execution directory, admitted input, and diagnostics
remain distinct supporting facts. An explicitly selected execution directory
appears as the canonical directory the child was born in, never the caller's
spelling of it. No receipt claims success over a failed
required stage. Ordinary Tell leads with the addressed selector and its
admission, naming an exact Turn once only when already known. Transport
correlation identity is not a text Turn address. Admission claims neither
submission, an answer, nor liveness. Failed and exceptional actions preserve
known admission, control evidence and cause. Kill preserves
each member's native outcome in frozen selection order instead of collapsing
partial failures into one verdict.

Opaque configuration and observation values retain exact names, scalar types,
collection membership, empty values, and provenance. Terminal-safe escaping,
including on Settings scope coordinates that need it, is reversible. Refusal
and recovery text keeps concrete causes, capabilities, and
coordinates. Text uses a stable shape and lets the terminal wrap naturally.
Layout never clips or splits a copyable coordinate, replaces a public
discriminant with decoration, or removes evidence merely to hide internal
terminology. Panels and receipts need no decorative separator. Full help and
exact content do not use the preview aperture; exact text preserves source
lines and indentation with terminal-safe handling where appropriate, and raw
reads keep their bytes.

Akuma panels place complete identity and known context before native work or
chronological activity, life, changes, and diagnostics. Coordinates appear once;
unknown facts stay absent. Status foregrounds retained activity age, never Turn
opening or inferred liveness, and renders the owner's bounded current-action
summary with exact detail coordinates. Status and World do not expand a chat
timeline. Thin catalogue and World rows share the native projection. History
and live observation preserve owner order,
selection, omissions, payloads, and attribution. Changes in the execution directory
take priority over separately summarized outside changes; unknown directory keeps
the unpartitioned summary. Identity, conclusion, changes, and actionable pending
or failure evidence stay visible. Diagnostics keep subject, availability, cause,
and recovery together. A timeline conclusion is not repeated.

History is immutable: it neither fabricates current state nor reads extra state
to fill its panel. Contract pages expose copyable journal addresses in append
order; optional Dispatch follows its owning record with its recorded time and
Akuma detail address. Page text stays compact, names its omissions, and never
implies current lifecycle from the slice. An amendment row names the event and
any retained retarget, not inferred changes to the terms. An exact Contract
read presents the complete retained historical record, including amended terms
and authored payloads, without terminal clipping or substitution by current
guidance. Unavailable Dispatch enrichment stays beside readable page rows.
Status consumes the same completion judgment as wait,
including outstanding input on a sleeping Akuma. Only an observed input wait
justifies elapsed-duration text; unknown change size never implies additions or
removals. Activity omits wall-clock timestamps while retaining witnessed tool
and wait durations.

Bounded free-text previews preserve multiline paragraphs and indentation, fold
single-line whitespace, and keep speech, input, thought, and action distinct without
invented quotation marks. A preview ends only at a whole-grapheme boundary and
names truncation. History previews keep the beginning;
status and World show outcome coordinates and verdicts rather than answer previews.
Akuma history exposes copyable report and Turn addresses even in narrow terminals,
with outcome payloads below preserving relative indentation. Exact activity text
returns full retained authored content or neutral tool summary without framing or
clipping; typed detail keeps the native row. Exact Akuma item and latest-answer
text remains raw retained bytes. Capture and retention limits remain honest.

Selected Contract status uses the same ordered facts across active and terminal
phases: complete identity, title and lifecycle; explicit holder observation;
supplied delivery identities; each requirement's native currentness, verdict and
stale reason; prerequisites; workspace appointment, availability and merge facts;
retained physical issues; and attached Akumas. Supplied summaries and abandonment
notes remain labeled bounded prose. Terminal state neither hides evidence nor
selects a preferred review, and gate evidence never establishes placement.
A delivered integration observed before target movement is identified as such.
Delivery preview identifies itself as a
dry-run observation, never delivery success. Candidate preparation, standing
requirement evidence, and target followability stay distinct. Known checkout
non-followability retains its path and cause without becoming a target-reference
placement refusal; target lag remains counted or explicitly unknown.

Progress notices, installation reports, diagnostics, and completion receipts
are ephemeral process observations, useful only when they report an actual
boundary — never simulated progress, persisted facts, or a hidden alternative
protocol. Four laws govern them. A command's durable output is one receipt,
plus refusals; progress is not durable output. Progress lives only in the
redrawable live frame and vanishes at completion. That frame is confined to one
physical row and may display less than its logical text. It never changes the
terminal's wrap setting or limits the subsequent receipt. Verification retains
bounded stdout and stderr suffixes for each observed phase and emits their labeled
blocks at the phase boundary, in stdout then stderr order. Each stream names
the amount of earlier output omitted, preserving the latest failure evidence.
An observed empty stream remains distinct from an absent stream.
Both terminal and non-terminal progress share that bounded observation;
output does not stream line by line. Only witnessed phase finishes supply
outcome and timing facts. Replacing or closing an unfinished phase flushes its
captured output without inventing a finish. Closing erases the live line and
retires its clock, never leaving a synthesized verification summary. Off a
terminal phase starts, stage transitions, and admitted facts produce no
progress output. The final receipt retains facts admitted before a failure
alongside the invocation's disposition and cause, without internal phase names.
A stop on a retryable
conflict names its retry class in that same outcome vocabulary, never a
terminal or substantive mark for a stop that concluded nothing. For delivery,
review, verify, and delivery preview, witnessed progress lives on the
progress channel while one complete final result remains on stdout; on a
terminal the only liveness evidence is a ticking status line, and a quiet
interval never claims that a command is hung or alive. Terminal cancellation
asks the owned operation to stop and waits for its truthful final receipt
rather than replacing it with an early exit. Verification progress names declaration
position and useful phase, timing, hook, or failure facts, not repeated opaque
identities. Native observations retain complete identities without becoming a
second result model. Its final receipt preserves partial admissions, retained
failure scenes, operational stops, and the ordinary completion judgment.

Spawn, ordinary Tell, and kill return one final action receipt without a live
observation frame. A Tell explicitly observing its input presents that exact
outcome rather than an admission-only receipt. On a terminal, pending Ask's replaceable frame leads
with the complete resolved identity and carries elapsed caller observation,
last-observed life, and the native bounded current-work summary with its
activity coordinate when supplied. Admission may start the clock before life
or work is observed. Each observation replaces the prior life and work; Ask
adds no second summary bound, reconstructs no timeline, and flushes no
unresolved rows. A narrow display may show only
part of the frame, without clipping the final receipt. The clock proves
observation time, never ongoing worker liveness. Off a terminal no running Ask
activity appears, and JSON mode opens no frame.

Ask erases its live frame on every exit. Its final progress-channel receipt
names the resolved Akuma, admitted input, exact native outcome and meaningful
control, wake or failure evidence. The exact input's outcome alone decides the
conclusion; last-observed life never substitutes for it. An answered Ask keeps
historical wake failure visible. Invalid output retains its native answer
evidence as a diagnostic, without becoming a parsed answer. Text stdout carries
only the parsed answer's JSON serialization once, without an appended newline;
other outcomes fabricate no answer. Shared final rendering composes those native
facts directly, preserving a raw serialized answer when no meaningful controls
require a receipt. Native history and streaming retain their complete capability.

Wait opens no live frame. Its compact projection preserves ordered member states
and identities without recomputing satisfaction. The shared presentation
keeps success, failure, cancellation, missing or unavailable evidence, and
unfinished work distinct. It labels an older conclusion separately and preserves
exact answer text. A running member can show only its witnessed compact current
activity or local activity failure. A group summary appears only when it helps
interpret multiple members or an incomplete observation; any elapsed time is
observation time, not liveness. An incomplete receipt includes a copyable
continuation when the resolved scope, identities and selection intent can be
preserved. Unavailable evidence does not justify dropping an unresolved peer
from that continuation. Process and transport edges consume the invocation's common verdict;
incomplete is a distinct process outcome but not a transport error.

Region overlap in bind and amend receipts is advisory evidence in native order:
each supplied relation keeps the related Contract and both patterns. An absent
relation means identical for equal patterns and intersection otherwise. Observation
unavailability remains distinct from known absence; neither asserts a code conflict.
Exceptional Contract execution preserves confirmed admissions, consequential
movement, resource ownership coordinates and the native failure boundary without
rereading authority or copying journal records.

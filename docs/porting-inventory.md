# v3 Porting Inventory

This inventory treats v3 as behavioral evidence only. The classifications use the v4 rules in `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/porting-policy.md`: lifecycle facts belong in `contracts/<machine-contract>.jsonl`, evidence is ordinary carrier-tree data, and derived surfaces reconcile after an accepted journal transaction.

## MOVE

- **Task document codec and pure task facts.** Move the coherent, storage-neutral parts of `src/core/task/document.ts` (`parseTaskDocument`, `parseTaskCreationDocument`, `serializeTaskDocument`), `src/core/task/model.ts` (`TaskDocument`, `TaskView`, `TaskDisease`, `taskUpdatedAt`, `advancedTaskUpdatedAt`), `src/core/task/validation.ts`, `src/core/task/board.ts` (`buildTaskBoardFromDocuments`, `resolveTaskReadId`, `requireHealthy`), and `src/core/task/tree.ts` (`buildTaskTree`). They enforce strict YAML front matter, preserve an opaque Markdown body, derive `blocked`/`blocks`/reverse relations, and report malformed documents without becoming a second authority. This matches the v4 rule that tasks remain ordinary tracked Markdown. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/task-organ.md` sections `Task Document` and `State And Graph`; `tests/integration/task-git-store.test.ts` exercises the file-backed surface, while its Git publication assertions are classified below.

- **Agent-facing task composition planning.** Move `src/core/task/compose.ts:parseTaskCompositionSketch` and `planTaskComposition`. The explicit `+`/`@coordinate` tree grammar, parent stack, dependency assignments, and check-only plan are pure planning behavior with a focused invariant. Keep `composeTasks` out of the move because it crosses into v3 repository mutation. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/task-organ.md` `CLI` subsection, and `tests/cli-expect/suite/50-task-compose.expect.test.mjs`.

- **Strict Markdown structure helpers.** Move the pure pieces of `src/core/markdown/lex.ts`, `src/core/markdown/parser.ts`, `src/core/markdown/sections.ts`, `src/core/markdown/render.ts`, and `src/core/markdown/titles.ts` (`lexMarkdown`, `parseToAST`, `parseMarkdownStructure`, `parseMarkdownListSection`, `renderNodeContent`, and title normalizers), plus `src/core/command-markdown.ts` (`parseCommandMarkdown`, `requireMarkdownTextSection`, `requireMarkdownListSection`, `requireMarkdownTitle`) and `src/core/region-markdown.ts` (`markdownSourceSections`, `parseRegionFragment`, `observeRegionSnapshot`, `renderRegionFragment`). They parse and render caller documents without reading Git or lifecycle state. Preserve their strict fence, heading, list, section, and lossless-body invariants; adapt field names to the v4 contract journal/input grammar. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/foundation/agent-contract.md`, `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/verb-consequences.md`, and the v3 task/contract document rules.

- **Provider-neutral terminal boundary and bounded event facts.** Move `src/agents/harness/provider-adapter.ts:ProviderAdapterBaseOptions`, `ProviderTerminalFact`, `ProviderTerminalError`, `createProviderFailure`, `createAbortError`, and `toProviderTerminalError`; `src/agents/harness/events.ts` schemas plus `parseTerminalFileLedger` and `foldTerminalFileLedger`; and the pure helpers `sha256Utf8`, `awaitProviderAbortSettlement`, `renderCapturedText`, and `linkAbortSignal` in `src/agents/harness/runtime.ts`. These establish typed completion/failure/abort facts, bounded provider payloads, and terminal file evidence without selecting a lifecycle authority. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/architecture/harness-public-api.md`, `docs/keiyaku-v3/architecture/agent-events-observability.md`, and `tests/fixtures/provider-adapter-contract.negative.ts`.

- **Provider registration shape.** Move the type-level boundary in `src/agents/providers/registration.ts:ProviderRegistration` and `defineProviderRegistration`, together with the name/schema checks in `src/agents/providers/registry.ts:isSubagentProvider` and `getProviderRegistration`. Registration is a finite adapter catalog, not lifecycle state. Provider-specific start functions remain a rewrite item.

- **Pure presentation primitives.** Move `src/cli/render/line-width.ts:displayColumns`, `truncateColumns`, `truncateMiddleColumns`, `fitVariableLine`, and `fitAffixedLine`; `src/cli/render/format.ts:shellQuote`, `formatAgeMs`, `truncateForDisplay`, `buildPayload`; `src/cli/render/path-prefix-compaction.ts:foldPathRows`, `renderPatternPair`, and `renderPathPreview`; and `src/cli/render/task.ts:deriveTaskDisplay`, `renderTaskListRow`, `renderTaskMutationRow`, and `renderTaskShow`. These consume supplied facts and implement deterministic width, truncation, path, and task presentation. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/command-output.md` `Shared command presentation` and `Task command output`; no listed symbol performs Git or lifecycle reads.

- **Generic Git process, object, ref, and status adapters.** Move the narrow physical boundaries `src/git/process.ts:openGitProcess`, `runGitProcessBytes`, `runGitProcess`, and `runGitOrThrow`; `src/git/refs.ts:resolveExactRefHeads` and `commitRefTransaction`; `src/git/object-store.ts:writeCommitObject`, `readCommitObject`, and `openRepositoryCommitReader`; and `src/git/worktree.ts:parsePorcelainStatusZ`, `getDirtyFiles`, and `assertCleanTrackedWorkingTree`. They preserve argv, byte, CAS, object reachability, path, and dirty-tree facts needed by the v4 carrier/reconcile kernel. Move the adapters only after removing callers that assign product authority to commit identity or detached objects. Evidence: `docs/architecture.md` `One Chain Of Truth` and `Transactions`; v3 `tests/integration/task-git-store.test.ts` and `tests/integration/repository-ledger-write-transaction.test.ts` demonstrate the useful CAS/process edges even though their higher-level owners are not portable.

## REWRITE

- **Contract admission and lifecycle workflow.** Rewrite `src/core/bind.ts:composeTaskBindDraft`, `previewBindContract`, and `bindContract`; `src/core/settlement/petition.ts:assertFoldedPetitionable`, `petitionContract`, and `buildPetitionClaimInput`; `src/core/settlement/claim.ts:claimContract`; `src/core/forfeit.ts:forfeitContract`; `src/core/renew-plan.ts:admitRenew` and `prepareRenew`; and `src/core/arc.ts:openArc`, `arcKeiyaku` plus `src/core/seal.ts:captureFoldedSeal`, `sealCurrentArc`. The agent-facing behavior is valuable: validate a contract, pin inputs, make one lifecycle decision, report committed/refused/contained outcomes, and reconcile workspace/delivery effects. The implementations instead depend on `src/core/repository-ledger/current-state-store.ts`, `src/core/repository-ledger/read-model.ts`, `src/core/repository-ledger/write-transaction.ts`, detached evidence, target/delivery refs, and accepted-tail replay. Re-express the behavior as pure journal decisions plus one carrier transaction and post-commit reconcile. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/foundation/agent-contract.md`, `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/verb-consequences.md`, `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/audit.md`, and the contract lifecycle integration tests.

- **Contract view and agent addressing.** Rewrite `src/core/contract-view.ts:renderVerticalLog`, `renderContractView`, `renderCanonicalCarrierBody`, and `renderCurrentContractView`; `src/core/context.ts:resolveImplicitCurrentContractId`, `resolveCurrentContractId`, and `readCurrentContractContext`; and `src/core/call/context.ts:readCallKeiyakuContext`. Their user value is truthful contract selection and readable lifecycle history, but they read v3 ledger/current-state models and treat carrier/ref coordinates as more than transport. The v4 versions must fold inline journal facts, keep evidence blob bytes out of decisions, and render only typed read facts. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/command-output.md` and `docs/keiyaku-v3/foundation/agent-contract.md` require the renderer to consume facts, while v4 `docs/architecture.md` changes the fact owner.

- **Task mutation, promotion, and settlement effects.** Rewrite `src/core/task/task-git-store.ts:openTaskStore`, `src/core/task/task-file-transaction.ts:createTaskFileTransaction`, `src/core/task/git-publication.ts:createTaskMutationCommit`, `reconcileTaskMutationIndex`, and `restoreTaskMutationIndex`, `src/core/task/task-contract.ts:previewTaskBoardForBind` and `replayClaimTasks`, and `src/core/task/compose.ts:composeTasks`. The Markdown and graph behavior is portable, but these functions make a task micro-commit and a lifecycle ledger construction receipt jointly authoritative, publish branch movement in the same CAS, and replay task settlement. In v4 task files remain ordinary tracked content; task mutation is a normal file/Git effect and never enters lifecycle state. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/task-organ.md` `Git mode`, plus the explicit v3 tests `Git mutation publishes branch and ledger together` and `Git mutation restores exact task bytes when ledger CAS refuses` in `tests/integration/task-git-store.test.ts`.

- **Harness-to-projection control plane.** Rewrite `src/agents/harness/projection.ts:call` and `revive`, `src/agents/harness/execution-handle.ts:createProjectionDriver`, `src/agents/harness/router.ts:startLiveProjection`, and `src/agents/harness/outcome.ts:processEvent`, `classifyFailure`, and `completeOutcome`. Preserve provider sessions, fork/resume, graceful-versus-force abort, bounded terminal causes, normalized activity, and outcome truth. Replace the v3 projection driver, callback/control wiring, persisted event assumptions, and projection-owned storage with a v4-owned boundary after the facts kernel is proven. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/architecture/harness-public-api.md`, `harness-control-plane.md`, and `tests/integration/projection-tell-wake.test.ts`.

- **Provider implementations and native event mapping.** Rewrite `src/agents/providers/pi/adapter.ts:startPi`/`forkPiSession`, `src/agents/providers/claude-agent-sdk/adapter.ts:startClaudeAgentSdk`/`forkClaudeAgentSdkSession`, `src/agents/providers/codex-app-server/adapter.ts:startCodexAppServer`/`forkCodexAppServerSession`/`settleCodexGracefulInterrupt`, and `src/agents/providers/opencode-sdk/adapter.ts:startOpencode`/`forkOpencodeSession`/`settleOpencodeGracefulInterrupt`, plus the corresponding `src/agents/providers/pi/events.ts`, `src/agents/providers/claude-agent-sdk/events.ts`, `src/agents/providers/codex-app-server/events.ts`, and `src/agents/providers/opencode-sdk/events.ts` mappers. The provider-specific behavior and safety mappings are valuable, including OpenCode V2 session events and Codex/Claude/Pi terminal causes. The v3 functions are coupled to `ProjectionDriver`, provider callbacks, session files, and projection lifecycle ownership, so they cannot move as whole adapters. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/architecture/normalized-activity-events.md`, `docs/evidence/named-provider-instance-inventory.md`, `docs/evidence/opencode-cli-tool-call-continuation.md`, `tests/integration/opencode-sdk-shutdown.test.ts`, and `tests/integration/codex-app-server-observability.test.ts`.

- **Projection lifecycle and liveness semantics.** Rewrite the transition behavior in `src/core/projection/generation/transitions.ts` (`launchIfSettled`, `adoptionIfCurrent`, `admitSessionIfCurrent`, `requestKillIfOpen`, `requestInterruptIfOpen`, `runnerVerdictIfOpen`, `replaceDeadRunner`, and `handoff`) and the read/actor functions `src/core/projection/generation/protocol.ts:selectCurrentGeneration`/`deriveProjectionLife`, `src/core/projection/projection-wake.ts:tellProjection`/`kickProjection`, `projection-kill.ts:killProjectionGeneration`, `projection-mint.ts:mintProjection`, and `projection-wait.ts:waitForProjection`. Explicit launch/adoption/session/intent/verdict phases, durable tell retention, adoption proof, no clock-based death, and truthful lost/failed outcomes are valuable. The v3 implementation stores them in SQLite `heart`, uses a leash/run-control protocol, and relies on persisted replay/control records; v4 requires a new projection design subordinate to the journal/carrier kernel. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/architecture/projection-generation-liveness.md`, `tests/integration/projection-supervisor.test.ts`, and `tests/integration/projection-tell-wake.test.ts`.

- **Projection activity/status/history views.** Rewrite `src/core/projection/projection-activity.ts:foldAgentActivity`, `foldPublicProjectionActivity`, and `latestPresentableProjectionActivity`; `src/core/projection/projection-summary.ts:projectProjectionSummary` and `projectProjectionStatusBoard`; `src/core/projection/projection-timeline-selection.ts:selectProjectionTimeline` and `selectProjectionSnapshotTimeline`; and `src/cli/render/projection-activity.ts`, `src/cli/render/projection-body.ts`, `src/cli/render/projection-history.ts`, `src/cli/render/status.ts`, and `src/cli/render/wait.ts`. The bounded timeline, stable event identity, explicit malformed/zero-activity facts, and answer-first terminal presentation are valuable, but the v3 read models and renderers are keyed to generation SQLite and v3 projection identities. Rebuild them as pure consumers of whatever v4 projection facts are later admitted.

- **Workspace, audit, and Git mutation effects.** Rewrite `src/git/branches.ts:createAndSwitchBranch`, `src/git/staging.ts:commit`, `merge`, and `revertCommit`, `src/git/detached-checkout.ts:materializeDetachedCommit`, and the higher-level worktree/evidence paths `src/core/settlement/claim-delivery.ts:buildClaimLanding`/`replayClaimTargetWorktree` and `src/core/settlement/evidence-pipeline.ts:evaluateEvidencePipeline`. Clean-tree checks, candidate execution, delivery, and verification remain useful effects; v3 branch names, detached checkouts, target refs, and evidence commit coordinates do not. They must run from the v4 transaction's accepted facts and be idempotent reconcile steps. Evidence: `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/audit.md`, `docs/keiyaku-v3/architecture/worktree-bootstrap.md`, and `docs/keiyaku-v3/product/verb-consequences.md`.

## DELETE

- **Repository-wide ledger and current-state database as product authority.** Delete the concept embodied by `src/core/repository-ledger/current-state-store.ts`, `src/core/repository-ledger/read-model.ts`, `src/core/repository-ledger/write-transaction.ts`, and `src/core/repository-ledger/accepted-tail.ts`, especially `publishRepositoryCurrentState`/`advanceRepositoryCurrentState`, `readRepositoryLifecycle`, `publishAuthoritativeTransaction`, and `resolveAcceptedLifecycleTransaction`. It has no v4 value as a second lifecycle store or read authority. Contract behavior moves to the journal blob and the v4 transaction protocol; carrier commit identity and ancestry remain transport coordinates only. Evidence: v4 `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/porting-policy.md` explicitly name the journal as authority and forbid current-state databases.

- **Detached evidence commits and evidence-parent topology.** Delete the detached-commit concept in `src/git/detached-checkout.ts:materializeDetachedCommit` (imported by `src/core/settlement/verification.ts`) and the detached evidence parent/registration machinery `src/core/repository-ledger/codec.ts:evidenceObjectId`/`evidenceObjectIds` and `src/core/repository-ledger/traversal.ts:readTypedEvidenceRegistration`/`traverseRepositoryLedger`. Evidence payloads still matter, but an evidence commit, detached parent graph, or commit reachability proof is not a v4 product fact; store the bytes as ordinary carrier-tree blobs and reference them from the inline journal. Evidence: v4 `docs/architecture.md` `Facts` and `docs/porting-policy.md` rewrite rule for detached evidence commits.

- **Carrier gates, effect journals, and generic replay adapters.** Delete `src/core/contract-carrier.ts:carrierPublicationGatePath`, `acquireGate`, `withContractReplayGate`, and `contractCarrierReplayAdapters`, together with `src/core/derived-replay.ts:deriveReplayObligations` and `replayCommittedDerivedState`. These mechanisms make derived-surface replay and a carrier gate part of the authority protocol. v4 has one atomic facts transaction followed by idempotent reconcile; it has no carrier gate, persisted replay queue, effect journal, or generic callback framework.

- **SQLite projection heart as durable lifecycle authority.** Delete the v3 persistence concept in `src/core/projection/generation/database.ts` (`generation_records`, `projection_tells`, and `projection_identity` schema plus `withProjectionHeartRead`/`withProjectionHeartWrite`), `src/core/projection/generation/store.ts:openProjectionGenerationStore`, and `src/core/projection/heart.ts:configureHeartBusyTimeout`. Projection lifecycle behavior is a rewrite item, but this durable SQLite/current-state boundary has no v4 value and must not be carried as a compatibility store or replay queue.

- **Renderer-side command-string inference.** Delete `src/cli/render/tool-command-normalization.ts:normalizeToolCommand`. It recovers semantic tool categories by parsing provider command strings at presentation time. v4 facts must be typed at the provider/facts boundary; a renderer cannot manufacture read/search/write semantics from prose or shell argv.

- **Provider evidence callback as a generic framework.** Delete the generic callback mechanism represented by `src/agents/harness/runtime.ts:emitProviderEvidence` and `ProviderAdapterBaseOptions.onEvidence`. Provider observations may still become typed evidence, but v4 should use an explicit owner-specific event/evidence path rather than a callback that can asynchronously fail, be ignored, or create an implicit persistence boundary. This follows the v4 prohibition on generic callback frameworks and keeps evidence publication inside the carrier transaction.

# Public Results

This chapter owns the structural boundary for public operation results and
progress observations, and the meaning of Contract admission, non-admission,
delivery preview, and post-admission work. Exact declarations and literal codes
belong to the producing owners and executable specifications.

## Structural Boundary

Every described verb retains its complete structural schema beside its producing
native owner. An owner also declares the wire answer used by structured
invocation consumers, sharing the same structural leaves with the complete
answer. Normality constrains nested required work as well as the outer decision;
it is a description of the owner's existing judgment, never a second classifier
or a runtime parse of trusted results. Observation declarations preserve the
availability and native state without proving completion over a selected set;
that judgment remains with the producer. Native callers and CLI JSON retain the
complete answer, including refusal, retry, and stops after confirmed effects.
An invocation Wait presents ordered member evidence rather than a separate
outer stop reason. Its single reporter, if any, keeps its position. The same
retained Turn outcome serves status, waiting and exact history: settlement,
identity, answer or known failure are native evidence, not a second result
vocabulary. A separately retained older outcome is not the current result.
Bounded Akuma activity snapshots share the producer's compact declaration and
native value across consumers. Exact activity
is a distinct typed result, not a single-row snapshot page; the continuous
typed observation is independent of that snapshot declaration.
An unavailable activity observation never reverses the wait's conclusion or
hides independently observed outcomes. Unobserved peers remain explicit even
when the group completes. A standalone transport publishes routing answers with
their producing owner's declaration, including incomplete Wait. It serializes
the native answer directly, without reparsing it or declaring a second result
model. Spawn also publishes a confirmed partial as a structured receipt,
whether returned or carried by an exception. This is successful receipt
transport, not successful completion of the stopped operation. Pre-birth
failures do not establish such a receipt.
Heterogeneous object answers
and nested observations identify their alternatives explicitly. Each catalogue
retains its owner-family identity through empty, absent, and unavailable answers;
World observation has its own family and is not an addressed-status alternative.
Missing
authority, unavailable observation, unrequested detail, and known emptiness are
different answers. An omitted optional collection, where used for known
emptiness, never represents a failed or skipped observation. An omitted catalogue remainder means the
observation is known complete. A readable-only catalogue keeps the rows it
proved readable apart from records omitted locally; its skipped count is
independent of the bounded row budget and remainder and never becomes a
placeholder row or inferred terms. When every selected record was omitted, the
observation is explicitly partial rather than known empty.

The owner emits its decision in that form. Creation and Tell admission distinguish
ordinary completion from a later operational stop explicitly; a normal completion
cannot carry a failed required stage. A stopped answer preserves irreversible
birth and admission and every independently observed failure in causal order.
Private custody may retain independent
facts required by admission, recovery, or another owner; it does not retain a
parallel result merely for presentation to discard later.

Thrown errors are not ordinary results. Distinguished SDK failures share
KeiyakuError; native failures and cancellation reasons may still escape lower
boundaries unchanged when no confirmed evidence needs attaching. Exceptional
evidence is readable directly from the thrown error rather than through an
adapter projection or hidden receipt store. An unborn Akuma publication
preserves its allocation, custody verdict, and leading cause as failure
evidence, not as a child receipt. Once birth or input admission is confirmed,
later failure retains those known effects; cleanup does not replace the leading
cause. Schemas describe the JSON wire form:
round-tripping a native result through JSON and its owner schema preserves that
form. Native Contract capabilities retain their process-local custody
without serializing it or reconstructing capabilities from JSON. Schema parity
is against their wire projection.

Schemas stay beside their producing owners for internal descriptions and transport
adaptation. The package root publishes native values and named domain types rather
than validators or a schema implementation. Transports consuming untrusted wire
values own their local structural admission without acquiring product authority.
Trusted local results are not parsed again. Shared semantic declarations remain
shared in composed answers rather than expanding a separate vocabulary for each
use. Contract observations publish one compact owner-adjudicated verdict per
selected gate. Addressed status also preserves each requirement's current
evidence and stable identity; stale causes belong with that evidence, not the
aggregate verdict. A current Verification position is additional display
information, not evidence identity. Model discovery may omit representational
constraints generated for output numbers without weakening native validation or
changing authored caller constraints and literal values.
Core does not depend on a schema implementation. The nearest
schema-using owner projecting a core fact owns its declaration; shared
declarations live below their consumers without cycles. Caller values are
validated once at their Library boundary. Invocation discovery describes directly
authored flat fields and leaf constraints; pure invocation admission owns their
cross-field applicability before coordinate observation. Structural validation
grants no product capability and never judges legality, admission, finality, or recovery.

## Invocation Answers

An operation returns its smallest complete decision-relevant answer, not its
input, journal record, effects log, or current Contract state. Accepted mutations
retain the resolved Contract identity and the operation's confirmed conclusion.
They omit ordinary successful mechanics and empty defaults. Detailed terms and
historical evidence belong to explicitly requested reads, not every mutation.
The journal, Heart, and Debt documents remain unchanged authorities; compact
answers add no persistent receipt, cache, or lifecycle state. The compact
observation producer owns its lossy text projection and visible clipping;
correlated retained activity remains independently exact-readable through its
native owner. Consumers share that projection rather than rebuilding native
facts or declaring a second result authority. Exact guidance and addressed Debt
detail remain complete native values. At the standalone transport edge they
remain complete unclipped shared text, without a duplicate structured answer.
Structured routing answers retain the native normal answer's detail,
discriminants, associations, and nested observations through JSON serialization.
Process-local capabilities remain outside that wire form. Addressed status,
thin catalogues, and waiting retain their producing owners' different detail.
Transport schema reuse must remain locally resolvable by the actual consumer
and describe the structured answer it receives. A Contract journal record keeps
its complete retained semantic content in both page and exact results. Historical
amendment records carry replacement terms; the change summary belongs only to the
amendment invocation answer.

The caller already knows the operation and supplied context. A renderer may
receive that context explicitly; it never scrapes stored facts to recover an
input echo or invents a second outcome. Amendment reports actual changed
sections and independently changed target, obligations, and prerequisites, rather than
returning the entire replacement document. Arc reports its admitted chapter
identity; its supplied title remains invocation context. Binding preserves its
native Contract capability. Delivery returns the captured identities; explicitly
requested preview is the sole presentation diff surface.

Classification belongs to the owner judgment. Invocation adapters consume it
without rereading authority or reconstructing required work from an incomplete
receipt. Acceptance, an answered unsatisfied verdict, refusal, retryable stop,
operational failure, and caller usage remain distinct. A terminal unsatisfied
check alone is a negative answer; an operational stop still makes the invocation
failed, and retains precedence over that answer. An exact Contract status read
that fails is operational failure; missing Contract authority is refusal.
A structured normal declaration
excludes required issues, unfinished checks, stopped testimony admission, and
operational placement or continuation while retaining ordinary waiting and
terminal negative verdicts. Optional retained residue stays
observable without becoming required work or reversing admission. Structured recovery describes
semantic capabilities and coordinates; only leaf CLI presentation supplies
command grammar.

A normal refusal or retry is returned only before the leading act has confirmed
an effect. A spent preparation is retired and prepared again within the
invocation's finite attempt budget; only exhaustion, identity collision, or an
unchanged-premise publication failure yields a retryable stop, which is not a
semantic refusal. The same condition during trailing work is a stop on the
accepted answer, never a retry of the confirmed leading act. Later stops remain
on the accepted answer or thrown exception.
An exceptional receipt retains the native category and cause together with
confirmed admissions, consequential movement, resource effects and issues, and
Verification evidence already produced. It does not reconstruct an unfinished
normal answer or preserve incomplete descriptive observations. Its category
retains the existing owner's judgment, and diagnostics retain the underlying
native cause through any evidence wrappers. Adapter
classification consumes these judgments without introducing another severity
authority. Ephemeral recovery snapshots survive accepted cleanup because a
later read cannot recover their unpinned identities.
A later successful read is not a substitute for evidence an invocation already
confirmed. Cancellation requests a stop; it cannot erase publication or skip
independently awaited retirement.

## Contract Outcomes

Delivery and review admit their leading facts and consume existing requirement
evidence during placement or dependent continuation. They never execute
Verification. Missing or stale testimony is unmet evidence, not an execution
stop. Satisfied review can find no delivery to place, and a later placement stop
cannot undo admitted delivery, review, or verification testimony.

Delivery identifies the captured candidate, integration, and content identity.
Review identifies the captured subject and reports changes from that observation;
neither result implies that the mutable worktree still matches it.
Ordinary fresh admission is implicit; continued admission is explicitly
identified from the historical entry witnessed in this invocation's custody.
That reuse is never represented as a newly admitted fact. A changed candidate
retains the content-change explanation witnessed during admission, not inferred
from a later read. Missing or disagreeing provenance remains an integrity
failure.

Placement has one adjudicated conclusion: placed, a semantic stop, or an
operational stop. Each stop family's producing owner exhaustively judges its
invocation meaning; consumers preserve that judgment. Ordinary waiting and an
observed target with identical candidate content are benign conclusions, while
retry remains distinct from failure. Verification testimony admission has its
own judgment: refusal or cancellation is a failure to finish recording, and
retry remains retryable regardless of the executed check's verdict.
The addressed result and dependent continuation carry the native placement
judgment without reinterpreting it. A stop belongs to the work it prevented;
an independent failure after a confirmed claim is an issue, not another
placement conclusion. Successful placement retains the destination and before
and after movement. A Contract destination is named as a Contract, not a branch or private
reference. Known movement survives later presentation failure. Unmet gates and
prerequisites retain their ordered actionable identities and distinguish
missing, stale, and unsatisfied testimony; already satisfied evidence need not
be repeated. Consumers never recompute currentness or eligibility.

Explicit verify reports the captured snapshot once and accounts for every
selected check that ran or stopped. Each check keeps its declaration position,
stable requirement when needed to avoid ambiguity, verdict or operational
stop, admission reuse or stop when recording is selected, and retained diagnostic
path. Each terminal result explicitly distinguishes execution without recording
from newly admitted or reused testimony. Execution without the `verified` gate
reports its results without recording testimony or requesting placement. Admission
reuse never implies skipped execution. No aggregate gate testimony is produced.
Earlier admissions, later completed checks, and cleanup residue remain visible.
A selected success alone does not establish whole-gate satisfaction. Ordinary
completion can place only an already-delivered Contract whose current
prerequisites and gates pass.

A continuation reports every dependent actually attempted, identifying claimed
dependents alongside independent semantic, physical, or operational stops.
Ordinary continuation does not repeat each child's complete movement receipt;
that detail remains available from the addressed child. Exceptional invocation
evidence preserves consequential movements already confirmed before failure.
A discovery or traversal stop outside a dependent attempt is an invocation
issue, not a dependent result. It preserves any earlier dependent results and
confirmed effects. Dependent ownership is explicit and never substitutes a
dependent's identity for the addressed Contract.

A normal integration conflict refuses without admission. Explicit conflict
materialization instead hands off the appointed worktree, captured handoff
base, target head, conflict paths, and actionable issues. It admits no delivery,
Verification, or placement. Dirty bytes require explicit capture authorization;
Git's private-index custody preserves the caller's real index and worktree
bytes. Recovery describes resolution of the final worktree bytes with dirty
capture, without requiring staging. Review may report observed unmerged paths
without making them a blanket refusal. Stale evidence preserves its native owner's
known reason and complete identities, distinguishing candidate-content and
snapshot changes from an unknown cause. Consumers never infer a Git cause,
recompute currentness, or turn successful execution into a failed check.

Region overlap is advisory planning evidence. Known absence of overlaps and an
unavailable overlap observation are distinct. Neither overlap nor observation
failure grants write authority, denies admission, or changes lifecycle legality.

## Delivery Preview And Observation

Preview is a read, never admission or a promise of placement readiness. It
observes a prospective candidate, existing requirement evidence, and independently
observed target followability. Preparation refusals remain explicit. Candidate
preparation does not establish prerequisite or gate satisfaction, and an
unavailable or unobserved target never becomes implicitly followable. A placeable
target may have a checkout that cannot follow the exact prospective movement.
Inability to follow is a checkout diagnostic rather than a ref placement
refusal; after acceptance it can appear as retained checkout lag. Disjoint
local dirt alone is not checkout lag.

Preview never executes declarations or hooks, admits facts, places, reconciles,
settles, or continues dependents. A failed or cancelled finite Git read keeps its
native failure meaning. The exceptional receipt does not reconstruct a partial
preview, and an interrupted target read does not become a target failure.
Standing evidence is judged independently for every prospective requirement by
generic currentness.
Requested diff and scope, including a genuinely empty diff, are presentation
data rather than journal, gate, or cache authority. Real delivery observes afresh.

Board and workspace reads are owned by [public-api.md](public-api.md), reset
semantics by [world.md](world.md), and reconciliation by
[git-reconciliation.md](git-reconciliation.md).

## Retirement And Failure Evidence

Resource retirement belongs to the invocation, independently of the current
candidate. Private resource names do not define public failure categories. The
producing owner explicitly distinguishes required operational stops from optional
residue; consumers preserve that judgment without inferring severity from a
diagnosis. Scratch cleanup, intentional failed-scene retention, and worktree
removal problems keep their diagnostic paths and known owner, candidate, and
requirement coordinates without repeating enclosing ownership. A process failure
keeps one bounded diagnosis that distinguishes exit, timeout, cancellation, and
truncated output. They add no journal fact and supply no cleanup or
reconciliation authority. Optional residue does not change the exit judgment;
a required operational stop remains actionable.

Reconciliation retains each confirmed physical effect once, in the order it
was observed, including effects that precede a later failure. Resource problems
accumulate across attempts and dependents. Finite Git reads report command and
frame failures at the affected read after owned command retirement. Later read
failures preserve known admitted work through the invocation's exceptional
evidence. Scope exit awaits already issued reads. An independent failure found
while draining accompanies the original operation failure. A consumer exception
retains its exact identity when there is no independent failure to attach.
Newer candidate conclusions cannot overwrite an earlier resource problem. A failure during
retirement keeps every previously confirmed admission and effect, including when
an earlier exceptional receipt was already attached to the native cause. That
boundary combines confirmed evidence rather than replaying normal answers.
It creates a fresh error with immutable receipt properties and preserves the
prior error and its complete cause chain. A conflicting admission claim is
reported as native corruption. The corruption receipt retains the prior
confirmed admission and independent new evidence without repeating the
conflicting claim. Independent invocations sharing a frozen reason retain
independent receipts without changing that reason.

Ephemeral progress is separate from the final answer. It may expose confirmed
admission before trailing work settles, but observer failure, gaps, or absence
cannot affect execution, custody, cancellation, admission, or cleanup. Only the
final owner answer adjudicates the invocation's conclusion. An exact input-bound
Ask answer retains precedence over historical wake failure; that failure remains
diagnostic recovery evidence rather than changing the answered judgment.
